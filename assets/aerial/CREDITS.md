# Aerial wallpaper clips — credits & licenses

The bundled clips are short (≤18s) segments of real aerial drone footage,
re-encoded to 480p H.264 (Main@3.1, low bitrate) so they play smoothly on the
Mi Box S hardware decoder and fit the <10MB RAM budget for video wallpapers.
