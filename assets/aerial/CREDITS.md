# Aerial wallpaper clips — credits & licenses

The bundled clips in this directory are short generated videos (20s, 1080p,
slow aerial-style zoom) derived from freely-licensed photographs. Each clip
was produced with FFmpeg (`zoompan`) from the source image below; no
additional third-party footage is included.
